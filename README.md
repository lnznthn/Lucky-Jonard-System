# Bodega

Offline-first stock, supplier orders and invoicing for a phone, backed by a Google Sheet. Stock goes in and out as movements. Claude can read a photo of boxes, shelves or supplier paperwork into rows that wait in the sheet's Inbox tab for review in the app, and each sale produces an invoice image to send to the customer. How it works is in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Setup

1. Open the Google Sheet that will hold the data. In **Extensions > Apps Script**, replace the contents of `Code.gs` with `apps-script/Code.gs` from this repo and save.
2. Run `setup` once and approve the permissions. It creates the tabs (Products, Movements, Customers, Suppliers, Supplier orders, Invoices, Invoice lines, Returns, Payment details, Inbox, Browse and Edits) and a connection key. Payment details is where the bank and e-wallet rows printed at the bottom of the invoice are typed: Method, Number, Name. Browse is a copy of every product with its numbers, for sorting and filtering. Edits is where product fields are changed from the sheet. After pasting a newer Code.gs, run setup again and publish a new version of the existing deployment (Deploy > Manage deployments > edit > New version), which keeps the same `/exec` URL. `setup` is safe to run again and never deletes data; on a 2.3 or 2.4 sheet it upgrades the tabs, columns and unit SKUs in place.
3. Run `showKey`, then open **View > Logs** and copy the key.
4. **Deploy > New deployment > Web app**, Execute as **Me**, Who has access **Anyone**. Copy the web app URL (it ends in `/exec`).
5. In the app, open Settings (the sliders button at the top right) and paste `<URL>#<key>` as the connection code, then **Test and save**.
6. On the phone, open the GitHub Pages address in Safari and use Share > Add to Home Screen.

The app works before step 5. Everything is kept on the device and sent to the sheet once it is connected.

## Using it

The same address works on a phone and in a desktop browser. A window 900 px wide or more gets a side bar (Home, Inventory with the number of waiting rows, Sales, Suppliers and Activity) and shows the lists as tables: click a column header to sort, use the filter button in a header to filter, click a row to open it. The side bar can be folded to icons with the toggle at its top, and the choice is kept on the device. A phone gets four bottom tabs: Home, Inventory, Sales and Suppliers.

- **Home**: a strip with sales today, sales this month and margin this month; two buttons, **New sale** and **Add inventory**; and one **Needs you** list. Each row in the list is a job that opens the place to do it, in a fixed order: entries the sheet refused, to-follow lines ready to ship (one row per customer), rows from Claude to review, products promised beyond supply, supplier order lines due, balances to collect, products with no price, units with no cost, and release dates to verify. A phone shows the first four rows and a "See N more" line when there are more than five.
- **Inventory**: search, filter by out of stock, short supply, no price, no cost, release date to verify or archived (the last tab, **Add inventory**, is described below), and tap a product for its four numbers (on hand, incoming, owed to customers, left to promise; owed is the to-follow lines on invoices and incoming is what is still due on Ordered supplier-order lines), details, the units on hand (oldest first, each with its SKU, its own cost and its condition, which can be typed or picked while the unit is in stock), history and edit. Units leave stock oldest first. A product is a model that can have many units, and each unit has a SKU such as `T0012-3`. On a desktop, Inventory is a compact table with every field, the unit cost and the stock cost; the headers of manufacturer, brand, product line and character open a list of tickboxes (nothing ticked means no filter), and release date takes a from and to range. **Stock in** and **Stock out** on the product's sheet record stock by hand, with one cost field per unit coming in; a cost can be left blank and added later. The **Activity** link on this page opens the movement log, where a movement can be reversed.
- **Sales**: the invoice list, with **New sale** at the top and the tabs All, Unpaid and To follow. Open an invoice to save or copy its image, add a payment, mark it Delivered or record a return (Restock puts the product back in stock, Write-off does not). A to-follow line on it has **Ship** (once stock covers it; the units leave stock under the same invoice number), **Change expected date** and **Cancel** (asks how much is refunded; blank refunds what was paid beyond the new total).
- **New sale**: pick the product (or tap **Sell** on the product in Inventory), check the price, type the customer's name and tap **Issue invoice**. That makes one invoice, takes the in-stock lines out of stock, records the cost of the units they took, and opens the invoice with **Save image**, which opens the PNG in a new tab to save or share, and downloads the file when a new tab cannot open. An invoice too long for one image continues on further images, which all download. Where the browser can put an image on the clipboard, **Copy image** beside it copies a one-image invoice to paste into a chat, on a phone or a desktop. A sale of a unit with no cost yet is allowed; the form says so, and the margin on that sale stays unknown. Each in-stock line shows the condition of the units it takes; picking another one there changes those units on the product itself. **Discount %** and **Discount (₱)** fill each other in, whichever is typed, and **Shipping fee** is added after the discount; both are optional and the margin leaves out the fee. Each product line has a **Pre-order** toggle, which starts on when the line asks for more than is free to sell (the stock on hand less what other to-follow lines are owed) and can be turned off to sell now, which can take stock below zero. A pre-order line is "to follow": it leaves no stock, counts as owed, and shows its expected date in the Expected arrival column of the invoice image. Its expected arrival is a dropdown of the open supplier-order lines for that product, or a typed date. While a line is to follow the form asks for the customer's phone or Messenger and shows **Paid now** (blank means paid in full); the invoice then carries Paid and Balance, and Home shows "Collect ₱X".
- **Suppliers**: two tabs. **Suppliers** is the supplier list (Name, Contact, Open lines, Units incoming, Note); open a row to edit it, or tap **Add supplier**. **Orders** lists the supplier-order lines (Date, Supplier, Product, Qty, Received, Cost each, Expected, Status, and Waiting, the to-follow units linked to the line). Open a row for the order with its lines: **Receive** per line with a quantity (it starts at what is still due) and a cost for each unit (pre-filled with the line's cost each), **Receive all**, and **Cancel the rest**. A short line stays open for the rest. **New order** opens Add inventory with Ordered selected.
- **Add inventory** (the last tab of Inventory, also opened from Home): rows that Claude has added to the sheet's Inbox tab appear here as soon as the app syncs, in a review with three groups. **Check these** holds the rows that need a decision (a similar name on file, the same new name on two rows, a release date to verify). **Ready** holds the rest of what will be recorded. **Needs a name** holds rows with no count or a name starting with "Unidentified", which are never recorded as they are. Tap a row's name (click the row on a desktop) to correct any of its fields before applying, including one cost per unit it adds. On a desktop the Cost cell of a row can be typed in place: one cost each for every unit the row adds. The **Check these** group can be folded, like **Needs a name**. A value typed there overwrites the product on file; values from Claude only fill blank fields. The correction is used for this review only and the Inbox row stays as Claude wrote it. **Apply** records the ticked rows and marks only those rows Done in the Inbox; unticked rows and rows that need a name stay there. A sheet then says what was recorded and what was left. **Dismiss these rows** discards everything shown. Above a Receive batch, a **Received now / Ordered** switch decides what Apply does. Ordered adds the Supplier (picked from the list, or a new name), Order date and Expected arrival for the whole batch, and Apply records one supplier order with a line per row (product, qty, cost each) and moves no stock. **Add a row** adds a row by hand, and **Order stock** on a product's sheet opens Add inventory set to Ordered with that product as a row. Claude can also write Inbox rows of Type `Order` for a supplier order or quotation, with the Supplier and Expected columns; a batch of them opens here with Ordered selected. Photos reach Claude either attached in chat or uploaded to a Google Drive folder, and Claude reads them with the toy-inventory-logger skill. When nothing is waiting, this tab lists the other ways to change stock: receive a supplier order (under Suppliers), change one product by hand, add a new product, or **Paste a block by hand**. For the paste, tap "Copy the prompt and product list for Claude", send that text with a photo to Claude, paste the code block it returns, preview and apply. **Count from photo** is for boxes and shelves, **Receive** is for a supplier's invoice or packing list, and **Movements** takes rows of dated stock changes.
- **Settings**: four groups. Connection holds the connection code and re-download. Invoice image holds the shop name, contact details, footer line and logo, and lists the payment details that come from the sheet's Payment details tab. Preferences holds the theme (Device, Light or Dark, kept per device), and the currency symbol. Data holds the two exports and erase.

## Working in the sheet

- To sort, filter or look through the products, use the **Browse** tab. It has one row per product with on hand, incoming, owed, left to promise, the units on hand (oldest first, each as its SKU and cost, `?` for a unit without a cost), the total cost and the number of units without a cost. The script rewrites it at every sync, so a filter stays and a sort lasts until the next sync. Changes typed into Browse are overwritten.
- To change a product from the sheet, add a row to the **Edits** tab: the Product ID, the field (pick it from the dropdown), and the new value. Leave Status blank. At the next sync the script applies the row and writes Done, or Refused with the reason in Note. The phone gets the change at its sync after that. To try a refused row again, fix it and clear its Status.
- The other tabs (Products, Movements, Customers, Suppliers, Supplier orders, Invoices, Invoice lines and Returns) belong to the app. They can be viewed, but they should not be sorted or edited, and Google Sheets shows a warning before a change to them. If one is sorted anyway, the script still finds each row by its Product ID or ID, so nothing is written to the wrong row. If a Product ID or ID is deleted or appears twice, the change for that row is refused with a message under Activity in the app and nothing of it is written; fix the tab and sync again. A Product ID on two Products rows also shows as a red line on Home.
- The Inbox is Claude's tab. A row with any Status, including Working, is not shown in the app.
- A cost is changed in the app, not in the sheet.

## Look and fonts

The app uses Bebas Neue for titles and key numbers and Geist for all other text. Both are served from `fonts/` and cached by the service worker, so text looks the same offline. They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; `fonts/README.md` names the source packages.

## Release 2.6.0

Release 2.6.0 changes both `apps-script/Code.gs` and the app. The invoice image follows the shop's template, with Condition and Expected arrival columns, the payment details at the bottom left and a totals block with SubTotal, Discount, Paid, Shipping Fee and a highlighted Total. Each unit has a condition beside its cost (MISB, MIB, MOC, CIB, Box damage, Loose complete, Loose; new units start at MIB), invoices take a discount and a shipping fee, and the payment details come from the new Payment details tab, so they never sit in this public repository. Data is not wiped. To go live: paste the new `Code.gs`, run `setup` once (it adds the new columns and the Payment details tab), type the payment rows into that tab, publish a new version of the deployment, and merge the app to `main`. The cache name is `toys-v2.6.0`. [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) lists every step.

## Release 2.4.0

Release 2.4.0 changes both `apps-script/Code.gs` and the app. What the app called an item is now a product, and each unit has a SKU (`<Product ID>-<n>`); the Product ID is kept in the sheet and not shown in the app. Stock is now Inventory, Category and Reorder at are gone, and the desktop tables, the review and the side bar are reworked. Data is not wiped. To go live: paste the new `Code.gs`, run `setup` once (it renames Items to Products, drops the two columns, writes the headers and the unit SKUs, and makes Browse again), publish a new version of the deployment, and merge the app to `main`. The cache name is `toys-v2.4.0`. [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) lists every step.

## Tests

```
node test/backend.test.js                 # Code.gs against an in-memory fake of Google's services
python3 -m http.server 8765 &             # the app
node test/mock-backend.js 8766 &          # the fake back end over HTTP
for t in test/*.py; do python3 $t; done   # drive the app in Chrome (needs Playwright and Chrome); common.py holds their shared steps
```

The browser tests read the ports from `APP_PORT` and `API_PORT` (default 8765 and 8766). All of these run against the mock backend, which runs `Code.gs` on a fake of Google's services. They do not prove behaviour on a real sheet.
